The Simpsons port 1.0.2: the players' reports on 1.0.1, and the arcade's attract mode in INTRO. Nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own
`simpsons.zip` (MAME's ROM set `simpsons`, 4 Players World, set 1) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of
it the first time it starts.

**INTRO > ATTRACT** (issue 22): INTRO on the menu has its page now: STORY, the story before stage 1 as before, and
ATTRACT, the arcade's own attract mode with no menu over it (its opening, the title, the demo and the high scores,
round again), till a button (A, Back or Start) brings the page back. Its sound follows OPTIONS' DEMO SOUND, as on the
arcade machine.

**CONTROLS on the Switch** (issue 21): OPTIONS > CONTROLS is there, to choose each player's buttons on their own
controller: pick the player, a control, and press the button for it (a button of another controller leaves it as it
was); DEFAULTS puts them back. The Simpsons Arcade Game plays with the same ones.

**RecompLauncher**: the computers' packages carry `recomplauncher.json`, so RecompLauncher can install The Simpsons
Mr. Plow Edition, ask for your `simpsons.zip`, make the game's assets of it, and show OPTIONS' settings on the game's
page. A shortcut can name the ROM set too, with `--game PATH`. Without them, nothing changes.

**INTRO's STORY** (issue 8): the story before stage 1, Smithers taking the diamond and Maggie with it; it plays by itself when
the menu is left alone half a minute, as an arcade's attract mode does. START skips its scenes, Escape or Back leaves
it.

**simpsons.7z**: MAME's set packed by 7-Zip works as the .zip does.

**The logs of the last three runs** are kept (simpsons.log, .1.log, .2.log), on Linux and macOS too; there, a
portable.txt beside the games keeps everything in their folder. simpsons.ini explains each setting.

**Speed** (issue 21):

- The picture drawn three times faster: a frame takes some 0.9 ms to draw on a computer where it took 2.8 (WIDE 16:9),
  with the same pixels, so a slow device has the game at its speed with far fewer pictures left out.
- Slow devices: where a frame takes longer to draw than its time, the game and its sound keep their speed and some
  pictures are left out, instead of all of it slowing down with the sound breaking up.

**WIDE**:

- Stage 2's end (a player's video): when the Krusty balloon falls and the floor gives way, the hole goes
  on past the picture's sides as one, where the sides showed pieces of its wall again. The fall off the cliff and
  Homer's fall down the gorge are 4:3, black at both sides, as they have no more picture than the cabinet's.
- The goons at stage 3's and stage 5's starts (a player's video): they wait past the wider picture's edge
  and walk in, instead of standing in sight at the 4:3 picture's edge. Stage 3's and stage 5's cards are centred as
  the others.
- Stage 1's opening: Smithers' goons running off with Maggie no longer vanish in the picture's
  right side; they run out of the picture, and the scene ends a moment later than in 4:3.
- Stage 1's shops: the restaurant's cook stands at its door again, no longer at the video
  arcade's in 20:9.
- Stage 3's elevator (issue 5): centred, the same black at both sides, from the platform's standing until the play
  goes on at the shaft's top (it stood to the left: 16 pixels in 16:10, the Steam Deck's, up to 72 in 20:9), with no
  jump as it begins or ends; the players stay on the platform as on the cabinet.
- Stage 2's Otto (issue 16): Otto is at his stall from the stage's start, while the family comes down in the balloon,
  instead of appearing as they drop from it (20:9, and at 16:9's edge). Stage 1's restaurant door, with its OPEN sign,
  is there from the start too.

**The rest**:

- BOSS RUSH's card (issue 18): BOSS RUSH, the boss's number, his stage and his name show on black while the game goes
  to each boss; with VSYNC ON the picture stood still on the character choice until the boss.
- The story before stage 1: one START skips all of it, straight to stage 1's card, its sound with it (the
  attack and the jump too, once the story has run a second). It skipped one scene at a time, the sound going on. INTRO
  on the menu still skips scene by scene.
- Bart's shirt (issue 14): orange too as he falls into the gorge at stage 5's end and in its water, and all of it on
  the walk to Channel 6; Maggie's onesie stays light blue when Bart picks her up.
- Barney's shirt: a salmon instead of the cabinet's green (stage 4, at Moe's); his hair stays yellow.
- High-DPI screens on Windows (issue 12): the game draws at the screen's own pixels where Windows scales the desktop
  (225 % on a 4K screen gave it 1707 x 960, stretched), its windows as large as before.
- The Simpsons Arcade Game with a controller: Back and Start held together two seconds quit it.

**Not tried yet** on a real device: all of the above was checked on a computer, frame by frame where the arcade's
picture and state must not change (the arcade game is the same as 1.0.0's), but not on a Switch, an Android phone or
TV, an iPhone, an Anbernic handheld, a Steam Deck or a Mac. Reports are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/cheviman/simpsons-port/issues).

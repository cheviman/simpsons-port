The Simpsons testing: the next release's changes, for the players to try before it comes out. It is not a release:
it is made again at each change, and something in it may not work yet. Tell what you find in the issues
(github.com/cheviman/simpsons-port/issues), with the version The Simpsons Mr. Plow Edition's OPTIONS shows at the bottom right
(V...-TEST...). As in a release, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `simpsons.zip` (MAME's
ROM set, 4 Players World) beside the game, and it makes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**To try**

- **WIDE, stage 2's teacup ride** (a player's report): the goons riding the cups, past the hammer, come again in
  WIDE; the ride was gone as soon as it was put in, so the cups turned empty and its goons never came.
- **WIDE, the scenes as 4:3** (the players' issue 24): a scene shown as the cabinet's picture (the attract mode's
  profiles, the stories, the ending) has black sides, as 4:3; before, a side whose edge was all one colour took that
  colour, so Bart's profile had a green bar on one side and a black one on the other. A backdrop of one colour at both
  edges (the menu's TV, the stage cards) still fills the sides.
- **Bart's shirt in the ending** (the players' issue 25): orange too as he turns to watch Homer throw the diamond.

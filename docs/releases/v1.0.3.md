The Simpsons port 1.0.3: the players' reports on 1.0.2. Nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own
`simpsons.zip` (MAME's ROM set `simpsons`, 4 Players World, set 1) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of
it the first time it starts.

**The attract mode after the Konami logo** (issue 23): left alone at power-on, the Konami logo is followed by the
arcade's own attract mode (its title, the characters, the demo, the high scores), as in the TMNT port; any button
opens the menu. Skipping the logo goes to the menu as before, and the menu left alone still plays INTRO's story.

**WIDE, the scenes as 4:3** (issue 24): a scene shown as the arcade's picture (the attract mode's profiles, the
stories, the ending) has black sides now. Before, a side whose edge was all one colour took that colour, so Bart's
profile had a green bar on one side and a black one on the other. A backdrop of one colour at both edges (the menu's
TV, the stage cards) still fills the sides.

**WIDE, stage 2's teacup ride** (a player's report): the goons riding the cups, past the hammer, come again in WIDE;
the ride was gone as soon as it was put in, so the cups turned empty and its goons never came.

**Bart's orange shirt at the ending**: orange too as he turns to watch Homer throw the diamond (issue 25), and
Maggie's onesie stays light blue when Bart is the one who picks her up after the diamond goes into Burns' mouth
(issue 27; her pixels were painted by hand on the frame, as his sprite there shares the shirt's colour with her).

**MULTIPLAYER**: a LAN game's end puts each device's own settings back into the game, not only into OPTIONS.

**The README** starts with what a player needs: what the game is, that you bring your own ROM set, and playing it in
four steps, with nothing to install; the command line and building it come further down.

**Not tried yet** on a real device: all of the above was checked on a computer, frame by frame where the arcade's
picture and state must not change (the arcade game is the same as 1.0.0's), but not on a Switch, an Android phone or
TV, an iPhone, an Anbernic handheld, a Steam Deck or a Mac. Reports are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/cheviman/simpsons-port/issues).
